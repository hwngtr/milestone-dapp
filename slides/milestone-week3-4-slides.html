The factory deploys clones and forwards the deposit

Implications
createCampaign() validates the deadline, goal, window, milestone count, sum, and exact deposit before it deploys a 45-byte EIP-1167 clone.
initialize() runs in the same transaction as clone creation, so no attacker can initialize a clone first. The factory forwards the deposit and never holds campaign ETH.
Limitations
A veto after a released milestone leaves refunds unfunded. The planned fix refunds only the unreleased remainder, pro rata.
The deadline closes funding only. A creator who never delivers leaves funds in escrow. A delivery deadline is Week 4 work.
Future work: Add the post-release veto regression, malicious reentrancy contracts, milestone fuzz handlers, and Sepolia deployment.
